⚡ RAPHINHA ⚡

Who is he?

Raphael Dias Belloli—better known as Raphinha—is FC Barcelona’s electric Brazilian winger. Known for endless sprinting, lethal left-footed curlers, and passionate leadership on the wing!

Quick Facts
  • 🇧🇷 Learned his tricks playing in the Restinga favela in Brazil.
  • 🔥 Became a cult hero in England at Leeds before moving to Barça.
  • 🤙 Ronaldinho is his childhood idol and close family mentor.
